\section{Synthesis: One Story Across Three Strands}
\label{sec:synthesis}

\paragraph{From the clustering strand (Section~\ref{sec:m1}).}
Students do form one stable habit split, study-focused against leisure-heavy
screen use, and the study-focused group scores 4.2 points higher. The same
group arrives with a higher prior GPA, and once prior GPA is held fixed the
gap is zero ($-0.01$ points, 95\% CI $\pm0.13$, test split). Habits travel
with a student's long-run attainment and add nothing exam-specific. The
analysis also showed that four broad GPA bands are too coarse to remove the
confound inside the lowest band, so any ``effect within the low-GPA band''
must be checked with a finer adjustment.
